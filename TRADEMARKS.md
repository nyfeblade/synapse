not cover the project's name or its look (section 6 of the licence says so), so this page explains how you
can use them.

The marks are:
- the name **Synapse**, as the name of this app and project;
- the **Synapse Bot logo and app icon**, and the Bot character drawn in the same style as the icon.

## Fine without asking
- **Saying what your work is based on.** "Based on Synapse", "a fork of Synapse", "works with Synapse" or
  "a Bot for Synapse" are fine, as long as it's true.
- **Talking about Synapse.** Articles, videos, tutorials, reviews and talks can use the name, and show the
  logo to refer to the project.
- **Unchanged copies.** You can redistribute an unmodified official release under its own name.
- **Linking to the project** with the name or the logo.

## Not without permission
- **Calling a fork or a modified build "Synapse"**, or any name that could be mistaken for it (such as
  "Synapse Pro" or "Synapse for Mac"). Give your fork its own name and its own icon.
- **Using the logo or the app icon** as the icon, logo or branding of your own app, fork, product or service.
- **Suggesting you are the official project**, or that the Synapse project made, endorses or supports your
  work, when it doesn't.
- **Using the name or logo in a domain name, app store listing or social account** in a way that looks
  official.

When you ship a fork, keep the `LICENSE` and `NOTICE` files, and say clearly that yours is a modified
version and not the official Synapse.
